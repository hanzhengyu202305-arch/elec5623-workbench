# Proposal outline - official 12-section navigation

Status: **official brief ingested on 2026-08-02; full working draft available**

Use `PROPOSAL_DRAFT.md` as the reviewable authoring source and
`ASSIGNMENT_REQUIREMENTS_MATRIX.md` as the official compliance record. This
outline prevents the working draft from drifting from the required section
order.

1. Cover and group information
2. Executive summary
3. Introduction and background
4. Problem statement and motivation
5. Stakeholders and requirements engineering
6. Proposed product and innovation
7. Proposed methodology and system design
8. Business and product analysis
9. Evaluation plan
10. Risks, ethics and responsible AI
11. Semester plan and team responsibilities
12. References

The official brief recommends 8-10 pages excluding the cover and references.
The final deliverable is one PDF per group, suggested filename
`ELEC5623_GroupXX_Proposal.pdf`, due through Canvas on `2026-09-08 23:59`.
Mandatory lab tutor approval must be recorded on the cover with tutor name,
approval date, and a brief approval statement.

## Minimum content checks

- At least three functional requirements per actual team member.
- At least five non-functional requirements.
- At least three design constraints.
- Testable acceptance criteria for the most important requirements.
- Direct alignment between requirements and the evaluation plan.
- An editable architecture/workflow diagram.
- Credible, verified, consistently formatted references.
- Transparent GenAI acknowledgement under course/University rules.

## External gates

- Actual group number, names, SIDs, and responsibility allocation.
- Week 3 interactive oral participation by all members; it is not graded and
  does not replace the written Proposal.
- Written/recorded lab tutor approval before the deadline.
- Stakeholder/business validation or a tutor-approved alternative.
- Tutor-approved corpus/annotation/freeze method.
- Lab 1 Azure/tool evidence completed by the student without exposing secrets.
- Compression, PDF rendering, visual QA, member sign-off, and Canvas submission.
