## Ten-topic own-words self-check

No model answers are stored here. Before the assessment, the student should be
able to define, distinguish, and apply each topic without reading a script:

1. how LLMs work;
2. the training process of LLMs;
3. tokenisation and tokens;
4. embeddings;
5. self-attention and Q/K/V;
6. causal or masked self-attention;
7. logits;
8. decoding and temperature;
9. fine-tuning; and
10. LoRA.

For each topic, independently practise: one plain-language definition, one
relationship to the end-to-end model workflow, one engineering implication,
and one common misconception. Actual oral questions and performance must not
be inferred from this checklist.
